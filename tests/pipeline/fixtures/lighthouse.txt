Chapter One
The Keeper's Hall

Mara pushed the heavy door and stepped into the long hall of the lighthouse. Rain hammered against the window at the far end.

"Tobin?" she called. "Are you up there?"

Her brother came down the stairs two at a time. He was pale and his hands were shaking.

"Something is in the cellar," Tobin whispered. "I heard it breathing."

Mara frowned. "It's the storm. Old houses make noises."

A low growl rolled up through the floorboards. Tobin grabbed her arm.

"That is not the storm," he said.

The cellar door burst open. A great grey dog, taller than a man, padded into the hall. Its eyes glowed yellow in the dark.

Tobin backed away towards the staircase. The dog growled and lowered its head.

Mara picked up the lantern from the table. She stepped in front of her brother and held the light high.

"Stay behind me," Mara said.

The dog flinched from the bright flame. It whined, turned, and slunk back towards the cellar.

Mara closed the cellar door and leaned against it, breathing hard.

"You were brilliant," Tobin said.

"Don't tell anyone," said Mara, and she laughed.
