CHAPTER TEN
The Troll

The castle corridor was cold and empty. Torches flickered along the stone walls as Harry and Ron crept past the statue near the staircase.

"Hermione doesn't know about the troll," Harry whispered.

"Then we have to find her," said Ron. He looked up and down the hallway. "She's in the girls' bathroom. Everyone says so."

A foul smell drifted along the passage. Something heavy was dragging itself across the flagstones.

"What is that?" Ron asked.

Harry grabbed Ron's sleeve and pulled him behind the statue. A huge troll lumbered out of the shadows, its club scraping the floor. It stopped at a door, peered inside, and squeezed through.

"The key is still in the lock," said Harry. He ran to the door, slammed it shut and turned the key.

"We did it!" Ron shouted.

Then a high, terrified scream came from behind the door.

"That's the girls' bathroom," Harry said. His face went white. "Hermione is in there!"

Harry unlocked the door and pulled it open.

* * *

The bathroom was a ruin. Broken sinks lay across the tiled floor, and water sprayed from a burst pipe. Hermione was pressed against the far wall, shaking.

The troll swung its club. The mirrors above the sinks shattered.

"Help me!" Hermione screamed.

"Over here, you great lump!" Ron yelled. He threw a broken tap at the troll.

The troll turned slowly towards Ron. It raised its club high above his head.

Harry ran at the troll and jumped onto its back. The troll roared and shook its head.

"Ron, the levitation charm!" Harry shouted. "Use it on the club!"

Ron raised his wand. "Wingardium Leviosa!" he cried.

The club flew out of the troll's hand and rose into the air. Then it fell, straight onto the troll's head. The troll swayed and crashed to the floor.

Hermione stepped forward slowly. "Is it dead?" she whispered.

"Just knocked out, I think," said Harry.

Footsteps hurried along the corridor. Professor McGonagall burst into the bathroom and stared at the troll.

"What on earth were you thinking?" she said. "You should be in your dormitories!"

"It was my fault, Professor," said Hermione. "I went looking for the troll. They came to save me."
