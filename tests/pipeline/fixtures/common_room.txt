Chapter One
The Common Room

The common room was warm, and a fire crackled in the hearth. Harry sat in an armchair by the window.

Ron walked up to Harry and patted him on the head.

"Cheer up," said Ron. "It was only a Quidditch match."

Hermione came over and hugged Harry.

"We're proud of you," Hermione said.

Malfoy pushed through the portrait hole, smirking. He walked up to Harry and shoved him.

"Nice flying, Potter," Malfoy sneered.

Harry stood up and walked towards Malfoy.

"Leave him alone," Ron shouted.

Malfoy backed away from Harry and fled down the stairs.
