Chapter One
The Harbour Inn

The tavern was loud and smoky. Sailors crowded around the long tables, and a fire roared in the great stone hearth.

Elena pushed through the crowd to the bar. She was soaked from the rain.

"I need passage to the islands," she told the innkeeper. "Tonight."

The innkeeper laughed. "No captain will sail in this storm."

A tall man with a grey beard stood up from a corner table. "I will," he said. "For the right price."

"Who are you?" Elena asked.

"Captain Rourke," said the man. He held out a rough hand.

Elena hesitated. Then she took his hand and shook it.

"We leave at midnight," said Rourke.

Chapter Two
The Storm

The ship rolled and groaned beneath their feet. Waves crashed over the deck, and the sail cracked like thunder above them.

"Hold the rope!" Captain Rourke shouted. "Don't let go!"

Elena grabbed the rope and pulled with all her strength. The mast creaked.

A huge wave slammed into the ship. A sailor slipped and slid towards the rail.

Elena dropped the rope and ran to the sailor. She caught his arm just before he went over the side.

"You saved him," Rourke said, staring at her.

The storm began to calm. Elena sat down on the wet deck, breathing hard.

"Welcome aboard," said Rourke, and he laughed.
